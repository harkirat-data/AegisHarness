# Aegis Executive Payroll & Compensation Policy

Classification: CONFIDENTIAL (Tier 2)

## Executive Compensation Framework
Base salaries for Vice President roles start at $250,000 with a 25% target annual performance bonus.
Severance terms for C-level executives guarantee 12 months base salary upon non-voluntary separation.
All payroll disbursements are processed via the encrypted SyntheticPay Cloud vault.
