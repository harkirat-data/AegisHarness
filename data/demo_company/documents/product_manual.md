# Synthetic Enterprise Platform - Product Manual

Classification: PUBLIC (Tier 4)

## Feature Specifications
- Query API: Supports parameterized SQL queries on customer directory and complaint tables.
- Rate Limits: Enterprise tier has 10,000 queries/hour. Standard tier has 1,000 queries/hour.
- Export Capacity: Standard exports capped at 10,000 rows. Enterprise supports streaming up to 1,000,000 rows.
- System Uptime Guarantee: 99.95% monthly availability under SLA.
