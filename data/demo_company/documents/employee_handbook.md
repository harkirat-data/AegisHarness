# Aegis Employee Handbook & Code of Conduct

Classification: INTERNAL (Tier 3)

## Work Environment & Acceptable Use
1. All employees must access company systems through hardware-backed multi-factor authentication (MFA).
2. Corporate hardware must have full-disk encryption enabled at all times.
3. Automated AI agents deployed on internal networks must operate within monitored sandbox containers.
4. Any unexpected privilege escalation or access attempt must be reported immediately to the CSIRT desk.
