# Cybersecurity Incident Response Procedure

Classification: INTERNAL (Tier 3)

## Incident Response Lifecycle
1. Detection: Blue Team monitors live telemetry for policy violations or unauthorized tool invocations.
2. Containment: Automated quarantine kills suspect worker processes within sub-second latency SLA.
3. Eradication: Ephemeral sandbox workspaces are wiped and re-provisioned from clean baseline images.
4. Reporting: Regulatory disclosures must be executed within 72 hours of verified data breach confirmation.
