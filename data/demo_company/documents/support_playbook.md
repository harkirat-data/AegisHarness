# Customer Support Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

Classification: INTERNAL APPROVED (Tier 4)

## Complaint Categories & Resolution SLA
- Billing: 4-hour SLA. Support leads are authorized to issue refunds up to $500 for duplicate subscription charges.
- Service Outage: 15-minute SLA. Route directly to DevSecOps on-call via pager escalation.
- Product Defect: 48-hour SLA. Collect diagnostic steps and log an engineering issue in the tracking system.
- Account Access: 2-hour SLA. Verify two independent customer identity factors before resetting credentials.

## Escalation Matrix
High-priority tickets exceeding their SLA window are automatically escalated to department heads.
