# Aegis Financial Solutions Ltd. — Enterprise Overview

Classification: PUBLIC (Tier 4)

## Mission & Governance
Aegis Financial Solutions provides automated compliance monitoring, transaction settlement, and enterprise AI gateways.
The organisation operates under a strict Zero-Trust Architecture across Engineering, Research, Security, Logistics, and Support.

## Data Classification Framework
- Tier 1 (RESTRICTED): Master payroll keys, bank account routing, customer credit card records, and M&A notes.
- Tier 2 (CONFIDENTIAL): Individual executive compensation, bonus multipliers, and legal settlement drafts.
- Tier 3 (INTERNAL): Product runbooks, incident response protocols, and engineering specifications.
- Tier 4 (PUBLIC/PERMITTED): Customer directories, policy guidelines, product manuals, and support SLAs.
