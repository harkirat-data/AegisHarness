# Leave and Attendance Policy

Classification: PUBLIC (Tier 4)

## Entitlements & Guidelines
- Annual Paid Time Off (PTO): Full-time employees accrue 20 working days per calendar year.
- Sick & Medical Leave: 10 working days annually. Medical certification is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.
- Parental Leave: 16 weeks fully paid leave for primary caregivers; 6 weeks for secondary caregivers.
- Approval Procedure: All leave requests must be submitted through the internal portal at least 5 business days in advance.
- Rollover Limit: A maximum of 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the subsequent calendar year.
