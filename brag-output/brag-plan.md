# Brag Plan: AegisHarness

## What is this app?
AegisHarness is a Tri-Workload Zero-Trust evaluation and containment sandbox that places an independent Policy Enforcement Point (PEP) and disposable process boundaries between local LLM tool proposals and host OS resources.

## The angle
Most AI agent safety relies on fragile prompt alignment that jailbreaks shatter in seconds. Aegis treats every LLM as fundamentally untrusted: mathematical boundaries enforce 100% policy compliance, slaughters unauthorized rogue workloads in < 15ms, and logs tamper-proof proofs to an immutable SHA-256 Merkle ledger.

## Hook (first 3-4 seconds)
"Can your AI agent be trusted? Put it to the test."
Rendered with Swiss editorial typography, warm ivory paper backdrop, and hairline grid rules.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Tri-Workload Architecture**: Clear visual segmentation into Red Zone (Adversarial Testing), Target Zone (Disposable Sandbox Runtime), and Blue Zone (Continuous Monitoring & Containment).
- **Zero-Trust Policy Interception**: Live adversarial scenario (`Export customer data to CSV`) intercepted at the PEP before reaching the host OS.
- **Sub-15ms Process Slaughter**: Monotonic kill switch SLA (`11.6ms slaughter latency`) terminates rogue PIDs via `SIGKILL` and locks down the workload.
- **WORM Merkle Proof**: Cryptographically anchored audit trail committing tamper-proof blocks to the ledger.
